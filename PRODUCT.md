# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Dealers and distributors** who stock and resell cooler motors and pumps, and need prices, steady supply and replacements.
- **Cooler manufacturers (OEMs)** who build motors and pumps into their own coolers, and need volume supply matched to their production and models.
- **End customers** buying a motor or pump for their own cooler.

Repair shops are not a confirmed primary audience.

## Product Purpose

The website is Xing-Z's storefront and lead channel for its range of air-cooler motors and pumps. Success is a visitor starting a conversation on WhatsApp (a quote, a dealer enquiry, OEM supply), since there is no online checkout.

## Positioning

Confirmed: every motor is tested before dispatch.

Not confirmed, so not claimed on the site (removed by the owner, October 2026):
- "One of India's largest manufacturers of cooler motors and cooler pumps."
- That the motors are copper-wound.

Also unconfirmed, but still shown: the capacitor brand, Tibcon, in the parts list (from a supplied photo of the part).

## Operating Context

Selling is seasonal around the Indian summer cooler market. Orders, replacements and technical questions run through WhatsApp; the site pushes every visitor there.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Range: eight product lines: high-speed, kit, 110, H Frame, exhaust, blower, capcan motors, and submersible pumps.
- Real product photographs exist for high-speed, kit, 110, H Frame, exhaust and capcan motors and submersible pumps (`public/products/`); blower motors have none yet.
- An exploded view of the H Frame motor (housing, end cover, bottom cover, capacitor from real photos; inner parts illustrated).
- Planned pages not built yet: 3D configurator, Why Xing-Z, Inside the Motor, Manufacturing & Quality, Dealers, Downloads, Support/FAQ, About, Contact.
- Hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages (`ArshGujral/xing-z-website`); changes go live only when the owner approves a push.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Xing-Z. Logo: the XING-Z wordmark with a lightning bolt (white, black and copper versions in `public/`).
- Tagline: "We don't just manufacture. We engineer."
- Hero: copper wires with small electric shocks, and the logo docking into a liquid-glass nav bar on scroll (owner's request).
- Both dark and light themes, with a switch.
- WhatsApp is the primary action on every page.

## Product Principles

- Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation.
- Show only real claims, numbers and quotes; leave gaps visible to the owner rather than filled with invented ones.
- Serve the trade (dealers and OEMs) first, without shutting out a single-motor buyer.
